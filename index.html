CHAPTER 01 — 2066

The Conflict

Russia faced political instability after the deaths of several major leaders. The United States saw an opportunity to gain access to Russian resources and technology.

Tensions grew into war. Countries around the world began choosing sides, and the conflict spread beyond anyone's control.

CHAPTER 02 — 2067

The Collapse

The conflict escalated into nuclear war. Governments collapsed, cities fell silent, and Earth became almost impossible to live on.

Humanity was on the edge of extinction.

CHAPTER 03 — BEFORE THE WAR

The Five in Space

Before the war began, five astronauts had left Earth as part of a new space-station mission.

Far above the planet, they continued their mission, unaware that the world they knew had disappeared.

CHAPTER 04 — 2068

The Rescue Mission

Five survivors on Earth discovered that the astronauts might still be alive.

With limited resources and almost no time left, they began planning a mission to bring them home.

CHAPTER 05 — THE RETURN

```

Something Was Still Alive

After years in space, the five astronauts finally returned to Earth. The planet they remembered was gone. Empty cities, silent skies, and endless ruins surrounded them.

They reunited with the five survivors who had brought them home. For the first time since the war, all ten stood together.

Then the scientist detected a strange signal coming from somewhere beneath the ruins of an abandoned city.

The signal repeated three words:

WE ARE STILL HERE.

Nobody knew who had sent it. Before they could investigate, an unexpected accident changed everything.

```

CHAPTER 06 — THE LAST SIGNAL

```

There Was Someone Else

The signal was coming from an underground research facility. The ten survivors gathered whatever equipment they could find and began searching for its source.

Inside the facility, they discovered an old computer that had been running for years. The signal wasn't a distress call. It was a warning.

Before they could understand what it meant, the facility's power system began to fail. The group rushed toward the exit, but an explosion shook the entire building.

The screen flickered one last time.

TRANSMISSION SENT. LOCATION: EARTH.

Then everything went dark.

```

CHAPTER 05 — THE LAST TEN

The End

The rescue succeeded. For the first time in years, all ten survivors were together.

But an unexpected accident ended their journey. Ten lives disappeared, leaving behind a silent planet and a mission that would never be forgot ```
